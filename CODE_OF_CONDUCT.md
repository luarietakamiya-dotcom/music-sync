# Community Code of Conduct

Music Sync welcomes creators and developers of all backgrounds and experience
levels. Be respectful, assume questions are sincere, and discuss the work rather
than attacking the person. Give clear technical feedback and credit others' work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate
disruption and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces
maintained by the project.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, request a correction, pause a
conversation, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation. They should
explain decisions privately where appropriate, protect reporters' privacy and
recuse themselves when there is a conflict of interest.

Report concerns privately using the maintainer contact listed in the repository
profile. If that person is involved, contact another listed maintainer. The owner
must publish a working reporting contact before launching the public community;
this local pre-publication workspace has no public contact yet. Do not post
personal details in a public issue.
